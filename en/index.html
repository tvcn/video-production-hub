STORY The One-Line Story: Who Wants What, and What Stands in the Way Compress a vague idea into a shootable sentence: a specific person, a specific want, and a specific obstacle. SHOOT The Exposure Triangle: Protect the Highlights First Shutter, aperture, and ISO are not three equal knobs. Set motion first, depth second, and use ISO last. TOOLS Buying a Camera: Budget Before Body On a limited budget, the body is not first. Find whether you lack low light, autofocus, 10-bit, or audio. The wrong body forces every accessory to change with it. POST Color Management: Put the LUT in the Right Place Color management is not applying a cinematic LUT. It answers what the source space is, what the monitor is, and what delivery needs, then puts the transform on the right node. CONTEXT Contract Scope: Price the “One More Revision” Before It Happens The most common film contract dispute is not payment; it is scope. Unclear deliverables, revisions, rights, acceptance, and termination become unpaid overtime. FORMATS Corporate Video Breakdown: Six Beats in Practice Composite sample: break corporate video into six beats and read picture, sound, and the pass line beat by beat; illustrative numbers are not client data.
