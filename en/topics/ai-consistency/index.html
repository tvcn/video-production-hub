Consistency and digital people

Character, scene and digital-human consistency: reference image, fixed description, consent and disclosure all matter.

20 guides · grouped by topic, not publish date

  1. AI / Consistency

    Character Consistency: One Reference, One Locked Description

    Generating a hundred faces is easy. Making the hundredth look like the same person is not. Lock the face, wardrobe, and set into reusable anchors before scaling.

  2. AI / Digital Human

    Digital Humans: Rights Before Resemblance

    A digital human has to solve likeness, voice, lip sync, gesture, and disclosure at once. Miss one and the audience drops out; brands and news should be extra careful.

  3. AI / Scene consistency

    One Room, Every Shot: Locking AI Scene Consistency

    AI is great at making a room that is almost the same: window left, then right in the next shot. Build a scene anchor, generate in camera order, and cut around the drift.

  4. AI Video / Consistency

    AI Character Sheet: Lock the Description

    Lock five fields: face, age, hair, wardrobe, temperament. Carry them into every generation.

  5. AI Video / Consistency

    Wardrobe Consistency: One Outfit, One Description

    Wardrobe consistency needs a fixed description and reference, not the words "same person."

  6. AI Video / Digital human

    Digital Human Voice: Consent and Range

    Get consent and define the range before cloning a voice. Capability is not permission.

  7. AI Video / Digital human

    Digital Human Gesture: Break the Loop

    Break the gesture loop every fifteen seconds: shift weight or take the hands out of frame.

  8. AI Video / Digital human

    Digital Human Lip Sync: Consonants First

    Align consonants first, especially p, b, m. Small vowel drift is tolerable.

  9. AI Video / AI audio

    Voice Clone: License, Scope, Label

    State license, term, platforms, and whether retraining is allowed; label it in the credits.

  10. AI Video / AI audio

    AI Music: Style, Rights, Mix

    Clear commercial rights before style, and mix it under the dialogue.

  11. AI Video / Post QC

    AI Post QC: Catch Generation Errors Before Delivery

    AI QC is not “does it look real.” Check in order: identity, motion, lip sync, text, physics, continuity, rights and disclosure. One failure stops the gate.

  12. AI Video / Beginner

    Reference images and first frames

    A reference is not a pretty image. It answers who the subject is, the ratio, where light comes from and what the background is. A first frame must also leave room for motion.

  13. AI Video / Workflow

    Multi-shot continuity

    Multi-shot continuity is not the same character appearing three times; it is face, wardrobe, props, light direction and motion direction not jumping across cuts. Lock three anchors before shot four.

  14. AI Video / Workflow

    AI product ad workflow

    The first rule of a product ad is no product warp: bottle shape, label, color and material must not drift. Make the product hero first, then generate the scene; not the reverse.

  15. AI Video / Tests & Tools

    First frame, last frame, reference test

    Image-to-video is not “does it look alike” but “does it still look alike when it moves.” First frame locks composition, last frame locks the landing, references lock identity; test them separately.

  16. AI Video / Tests & Tools

    Multi-shot consistency test

    Consistency is not a single-frame likeness. Across three cuts, check whether face, wardrobe, hair, props and light still belong to the same person/set.

  17. AI Video / Scenario

    Ecommerce and product catalog

    Ecommerce AI video is not about beauty; it is product truth, a clear benefit and platform readiness. One 15s hero, one 30s detail and three 6s ad cuts share one product plate.

  18. AI Video / Scenario

    Douyin, Reels, TikTok, Shorts

    All four are vertical, but pace, subtitle safe area, cover and duration differ. 15s one hook, 30s one turn, 60s one story; do not cross-post one cut.

  19. AI Video / Scenario

    Corporate video and training

    Corporate video's difficulty is approval, not generation: brand, legal, HR, product and compliance all edit. Make an approvable storyboard first, then replaceable shots.

  20. AI Video / Scenario

    Education and explainer video

    Knowledge video must be correct first, clear second. AI fits demo animation, analogy scenes and transitions; it should not replace the teacher on core concepts.
