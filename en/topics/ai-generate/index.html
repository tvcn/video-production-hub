TOPIC / ai

Generation and shots

Text-to-video, image-to-video, first and last frames, motion control: state the constraints before letting the model improvise.

20 guides · grouped by topic, not publish date

  1. AI / Consistency

    Character Consistency: One Reference, One Locked Description

    Generating a hundred faces is easy. Making the hundredth look like the same person is not. Lock the face, wardrobe, and set into reusable anchors before scaling.

  2. AI / Image-to-Video

    Image-to-Video: First Frame, Last Frame, Motion

    Get one image right, then make it move. Lock the first and last frame; the motion in between settles itself. No prompt length can replace those two frames.

  3. AI / Look

    Why AI Footage Looks Plastic: Light, Skin, Lens

    Most “AI look” comes from three places: light from everywhere, skin that is too clean, and a lens that is too perfect. Fix those first.

  4. AI / Scene consistency

    One Room, Every Shot: Locking AI Scene Consistency

    AI is great at making a room that is almost the same: window left, then right in the next shot. Build a scene anchor, generate in camera order, and cut around the drift.

  5. AI Video / Generation

    Text to Video: Write Shots, Not Adjectives

    Write shots and actions, not "cinematic, epic"; the model cannot act on adjectives.

  6. AI Video / Ethics

    Model Comparison: Test by Shot Type

    Do not compare leaderboards; test by shot type: face, hands, motion, text, long take.

  7. AI Video / Generation

    Motion Control: Give the Camera a Route

    Give the camera a route: where it starts, what it passes, where it stops. "Camera moves" says nothing.

  8. AI Video / Generation

    Negative Prompt: Remove Before You Add

    Remove what ruins the frame first: flicker, extra fingers, garbled text, face morphing.

  9. AI Video / Generation

    Camera Prompt: Framing, Angle, Lens

    Camera prompts need framing, angle, and lens. Without them, the model guesses.

  10. AI Video / Generation

    Prompt Structure: Subject, Action, Space, Camera

    Build prompts in four parts: who, doing what, where, camera. Stable order gives stable output.

  11. AI Video / Generation

    AI Shot List: One Prompt, One Shot

    One prompt, one shot. Three events in one prompt become a random montage.

  12. AI Video / Consistency

    AI Character Sheet: Lock the Description

    Lock five fields: face, age, hair, wardrobe, temperament. Carry them into every generation.

  13. AI Video / Hybrid workflow

    Hybrid Workflow: Lock Live Action First

    Lock live action before generating missing shots. Generate first and the light will never match.

  14. AI Video / Ethics

    AI Disclosure: Visible, Specific, Honest

    Disclosure must be visible, specific, and say what was generated; buried at the end is no disclosure.

  15. AI Video / Post QC

    AI Post QC: Catch Generation Errors Before Delivery

    AI QC is not “does it look real.” Check in order: identity, motion, lip sync, text, physics, continuity, rights and disclosure. One failure stops the gate.

  16. AI Video / Models & Cost

    AI video model radar: November 2026

    Do not pick a model by leaderboard. Pick by shot type, duration, audio, region and budget. Seven November 2026 candidates, each with a clear boundary.

  17. AI Video / Model dossier

    Veo 3.1: what it is good for

    Veo 3.1 is built around 4–8s clips, native audio, 4K and start/end frames. It fits high-value short shots, not low-cost batch work.

  18. AI Video / Model dossier

    Kling 3.x / 4.0: people, motion and 4K

    Kling 4.0 extends single-pass duration to 30s with 10 keyframes and 4K; Kling 3.0 remains the cheaper 15s multi-shot option. People and motion are its home turf.

  19. AI Video / Model dossier

    Seedance 2.5: multi-shot, audio and price

    Seedance 2.5 supports 30s single-pass generation, up to 30 images/10 videos/10 audio references, and timestamp-level region editing. It fits multi-shot narrative and ad iteration.

  20. AI Video / Model dossier

    Runway Gen-4.5: camera control and workflow

    Gen-4.5 is a 2–10s, 720p text/image-to-video model strong in camera choreography, sequenced actions and Aleph 2.0 editing. It is not a direct 4K delivery source.
