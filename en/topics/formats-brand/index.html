TOPIC / formats

Ads and brands

Product ads, brand films, corporate videos: a three-second problem, ten seconds of proof, and one believable closing line.

9 guides · grouped by topic, not publish date

  1. Formats / Product Ad

    Product Ad: Hook in Three Seconds, Explain in Ten

    A product ad is not a product showcase. Give a problem in three seconds, one benefit in the middle, one action at the end.

  2. Formats / Brand Film

    Brand Film: Make Them Believe One Sentence

    A brand film does not explain specs; it leaves one attitude. The product is evidence, not the protagonist.

  3. Formats / Product ad

    Product Ad Breakdown: Problem Becomes Proof

    A nine-beat breakdown of a sixty-second product ad: problem, failed fix, product, demo, proof, objection, second proof, result, action.

  4. Formats / Product ad

    Product Ad Template: Three Seconds of Problem, Ten of Proof

    Three seconds of problem, ten of proof, five of action.

  5. Formats / Brand film

    Brand Film Breakdown: Build a World in Ninety Seconds

    A nine-beat breakdown of a ninety-second brand film: world, character, want, obstacle, turn, action, resolve, line, hold.

  6. Formats / Brand film

    Brand Film Template: Stance, Evidence, Landing

    Stance, evidence, landing. The stance must be arguable and the evidence concrete.

  7. Formats / Corporate

    Corporate Video Structure: Six Beats

    Corporate video is not a company profile. Write a question the viewer wants answered, then prove with one person and one action that the company solves it.

  8. Formats / Corporate / Breakdown

    Corporate Video Breakdown: Six Beats in Practice

    Composite sample: break corporate video into six beats and read picture, sound, and the pass line beat by beat; illustrative numbers are not client data.

  9. Formats / Corporate / Template

    Corporate Video Template: One Sheet to Shoot

    A one-sheet corporate video plan: six beats, picture, sound, duration, and pass line; fill it before rolling and run the set from it.
