Short form and live

Judgement：Vertical drama, vlog, tutorial, live and podcast: give a reason in the first three seconds, then earn the stay.

14 guides · ordered by problem, not publish date

Read these five first

  1. Start: frame itDoes the Cover Still Read at 200 Pixels?

    On a phone, a cover is the size of a thumb. Secure one face, one action, one contrast before adding type.

  2. Next: choose the moveVertical Drama: A Want in the First Three Seconds

    Vertical drama has no time for a world. Put a want in the first frame, an obstacle by the third second, and leave one question unanswered.

  3. Then: failure modesVlog Structure: Give the Place a Reason

    Give the place a reason before making it pretty; without reason, scenery is a screensaver.
