TOPIC / main

Craft and judgement

From defining the problem to accepting the deliverable: decide what the film must solve, choose the method, and close with a checkable result.

20 guides · grouped by topic, not publish date

  1. Main / Learning path

    Edit, Grade & Sound

    A reusable post pipeline from ingest to delivery: structure first, sound second, grade last, then a technical check.

  2. Main / Learning path

    Media Literacy

    Judge a video in a fixed order: source, edit, context, money, AI traces, charts, rights. Pause before concluding.

  3. Main / Method card

    You Only Have a Vague Idea

    Do not write a script from a vague idea; write three lines: who wants what, what blocks them, what losing costs.

  4. Main / Method card

    You Cannot Write a Script

    If you cannot write a script, board eight shots with one action each; action will force the lines.

  5. Main / Method card

    You Cannot Find Actors

    No actors? Shoot hands, backs, objects, and sound; a person can be complete without a face.

  6. Main / Method card

    You Only Have One Room

    One room works if it changes once during the film: messier, emptier, darker.

  7. Main / Method card

    You Have No Lights

    Use window, white wall, reflector, and phone light; find direction before intensity.

  8. Main / Method card

    Your Budget Is Zero

    Cut scale, not structure; a simple film you can finish beats a better one you cannot.

  9. Main / Method card

    You Are Shooting Alone

    Use a locked camera and continuous action; do not act, operate, and pull focus at once.

  10. Main / Method card

    You Only Have a Phone

    Lock exposure and white balance, then stabilize the phone; steady image and sound beat lenses.

  11. Main / Method card

    The Image Looks Flat

    Flat images usually lack directional light and separated layers, not a better camera.

  12. Main / Method card

    The Shadows Are Dirty

    Lower ISO, add ambient light, and do not lift shadows hard; lifted black is noise.

  13. Main / Method card

    The Highlights Are Gone

    Stop down, add ND, kill unnecessary practicals; clipped pixels do not come back.

  14. Main / Method card

    The Color Is Off

    Fix white balance and mixed light before color wheels; grading a bad source gets dirtier.

  15. Main / Method card

    The Audio Has Hiss

    Check room, air conditioning, cables, and gain; replace the mic last.

  16. Main / Method card

    Wind Noise Outside

    Use a windshield and find shelter; post cannot recover words eaten by wind.

  17. Main / Method card

    The Actor Is Stiff

    Give a concrete task and make them walk; movement loosens lines.

  18. Main / Method card

    The Lines Sound Read

    Cut explanation, add action, and give them a listener; more adjectives make it worse.

  19. Main / Method card

    A Scene Has No Change

    If a scene changes nothing, cut it or let someone gain or lose something.

  20. Main / Method card

    The Opening Is Too Slow

    Cut the first thirty seconds and start where someone is already acting; context can wait.
