Color and compositing

Judgement：Unify first, stylise second; make both layers believe the same light before adding effects.

14 guides · ordered by problem, not publish date

Read these five first

  1. Start: frame itThe Rough Cut: Make the Film Work First

    A rough cut does one thing: let a stranger understand who wants what and what blocks them. Rhythm, grade, and score come later.

  2. Next: choose the moveColor: Match First, Style Second

    Fix white balance, exposure, and shot matching before LUTs and looks. If shots do not match, more style only makes it messier.

  12. Post / HDR

    HDR End to End: Do Not Just Brighten at the End

    HDR is not brightening SDR. It preserves one luminance relationship from capture through monitoring, grading, and delivery. Guess in SDR at any stage and the result is bright but flat.

  13. Post / Archive & reuse

    Archive and Asset Reuse: Make the Next Film Faster

    Archiving is not dragging a folder onto a drive. It lets the next person open the project, find media, know the rights, and restore grade and sound. Reuse is indexed retrieval, not digging through old films.

    Colour pipeline: management, primary, LUT

    Colour is not a LUT; wrong order of management, primary and LUT makes it dirtier.
