Location sound

Judgement：Mic position, room tone, wireless and backup: once sound is broken, pictures are hard to rescue.

10 guides · ordered by problem, not publish date

Read these five first

  1. Start: frame itWrite a Shot List Someone Else Can Run

    A shot list says what comes first, how long it takes, and what happens if it fails. A wish list says everything at once.

  2. Next: choose the moveContinuity: Every Take Has to Cut

    Continuity is not timecode. It is what the audience will notice: a cup switching hands, a button undone, sunlight jumping to the other side. One extra note on set saves a day in the edit.

  3. Then: failure modesSet Safety: Cables, Heights, Roads

    Cables, heights, traffic: check those three before anything else on set.

  4. Check: prove itWireless Lav: Hide It, Back It Up

    Hide the lav, secure it, and record a backup track before worrying about brand.

  5. Cross-read: one moreBoom Operator: Follow the Action, Not the Line

    The boom follows action, not lines. Lines stop; bodies keep moving.
